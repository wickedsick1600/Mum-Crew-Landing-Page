Good to know

Questions parents ask before checkout.

Is it completely leak-proof?+

It is spill-resistant rather than fully watertight. The lid seals when closed to help prevent a knocked-over cup from emptying into a bag, but it should not be stored or shaken upside down.

Can it go in the dishwasher?+

The cup body is top-rack dishwasher safe. Hand-washing the lid and straw helps keep the seal in good condition for longer.

What comes with the cup?+

The single CrewCup includes the 20oz stainless-steel cup, spill-resistant lid, custom-fit straw and a straw cleaning brush.

How long will drinks stay cold?+

The double-wall stainless-steel construction is described as keeping drinks cold for over 24 hours.
